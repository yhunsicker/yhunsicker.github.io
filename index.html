Problem's actually solved

Built in collaboration with AI and personally tested against accessibility best practices, every single line (some haveing been revised more times than I'd like to admit, but that's between me and my code.). Built with love and stolen pockets of time — reach out to chat about the work I've done, the work I'm doing, or just to say "hi" 👋.

  • The Commonwealth of Massachusetts | Mass Digital Services (MDS)

    MyMassGov Website

    One login. 25,000+ Massachusetts residents. Every state service they depend on. When Mass Digital Services built a single sign-on platform to connect residents to government services regardless of ability, device, or assistive technology. I worked alongside a cross-functional team to make sure accessibility barriers were identified and resolved before launch, not after.

  • fiveBelow

    Shopping Cart Redesign

    Users weren't completing their purchases. The drop-off wasn't a payment problem or a price problem — it was a user experience problem. I dug into the "why", conducting research that uncovered where trust broke down and shaped the redesign that fixed it.

  • The Department of Family and Medical Leave (DFML)

    Paid Family and Medical Leave

    People filing for paid leave aren't are often navigating a crisis. I designed WCAG 2.1 AA-compliant features for Massachusetts' Paid Family & Medical Leave program, turning dense requirements into a seamless experiences. Since launch, the program has paid out $1 billion in benefits.
